Summary: TechIT Network (a product of HaviTec LTD) is committed to protecting your personal data. We collect only what we need, we never sell your data, and you have full control over your information at any time. This policy is fully compliant with the EU GDPR, US CCPA, Nigerian NDPA, and South Africa POPIA.

1

Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly, information generated automatically through your use of our platform, and in some cases, information from third parties.

  • Account Information: Full name, email address, and profile details provided during registration
  • Startup & Business Data: Idea descriptions, stage, sector, strategy documents, and responses to platform prompts — all submitted voluntarily by you
  • Usage Data: Feature activity, session duration, pages visited, and interaction patterns collected automatically
  • Device Information: IP address, browser type, device identifiers, and operating system
  • Payment Information: Billing data processed securely through third-party payment providers — we do not store raw card numbers
  • Third-Party Sign-In: If you use Google or LinkedIn login, we receive your name and email from those providers
2

How We Use Your Information

We use your data only for legitimate and clearly defined purposes:

  • Providing, operating, and improving the TechIT platform and its AI-assisted features
  • Personalizing your experience based on your startup stage, sector, and preferences
  • Sending product updates, feature announcements, and support communications
  • Performing AI-assisted validation, scoring, and analysis on data you voluntarily submit
  • Ensuring platform security, detecting fraud, and preventing abuse
  • Complying with our legal obligations across all jurisdictions in which we operate

We do not use your data for advertising profiling or sell it to third parties for marketing purposes.

3

Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR — EU Users)

If you are located in the European Union, we process your personal data under the following lawful bases:

  • Consent: Where you have explicitly agreed to processing — for example, marketing emails or cookie tracking
  • Contract Performance: Where processing is necessary to deliver the services you have signed up for
  • Legitimate Interests: For platform improvement, security monitoring, and fraud prevention — where our interests do not override your rights
  • Legal Obligation: Where we are required to process data by applicable law or court order

You may withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal. To withdraw, visit your account settings or contact us directly.

5

Do Not Sell My Data (California — CCPA)

TechIT Network does not sell your personal information to third parties for monetary compensation. California residents have the right to:

  • Know what personal information is collected, used, shared, or sold
  • Opt out of any potential sale of personal information (we do not sell — this right is provided for full transparency)
  • Request deletion of personal information held by us
  • Receive equal service and pricing whether or not you exercise your privacy rights

California residents may submit requests to: anthony@techitnetwork.com

6

Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. Limited sharing occurs only in the following circumstances:

  • Service Providers: We share data with trusted providers (hosting, analytics, email delivery, payment processing) under strict Data Processing Agreements that bind them to confidentiality and security obligations
  • Legal Requirements: Where required by applicable law, court order, or a regulatory authority with jurisdiction over our operations
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, users will be notified with at least 30 days advance notice and provided options to delete their data
7

International Data Transfers

As a globally accessible platform, your data may be processed in countries outside your home jurisdiction. We protect all cross-border transfers through:

  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission for EU data transfers
  • Compliance with the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where applicable
  • Regional infrastructure strategy: EU user data is processed on EU-hosted servers; African user data is processed on regional or EU-equivalent infrastructure where feasible
  • Signed Data Processing Agreements with all third-party processors who receive user data
8

Data Security

We implement industry-standard technical and organizational measures to protect your data:

  • End-to-end encryption for sensitive data in transit (TLS 1.2 or higher) and at rest (AES-256)
  • Role-Based Access Control — only authorized personnel with a specific need can access user data
  • JWT-based authentication with session management and automatic expiry
  • Row-Level Security policies in our database infrastructure
  • Breach detection systems with a 72-hour notification protocol for GDPR-covered incidents
  • Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments

If you believe your account has been compromised, contact us immediately at havitecltd@gmail.com. We treat all security reports as priority.

9

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

TechIT Network uses cookies and similar technologies. The types we use:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for the platform to function. These cannot be disabled without breaking core features (session management, authentication)
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how users interact with the platform so we can improve it. Collected in aggregate, anonymized form
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your language, interface settings, and customizations

EU users are presented with a cookie consent banner on first access. You may manage, change, or withdraw cookie consent at any time through your browser settings or the cookie preference center within your account settings.

10

Data Retention

We retain your personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy or as required by law:

  • Active account data: Retained for the duration of your account and up to 30 days after deletion request
  • Anonymized usage data: May be retained indefinitely for platform analytics and improvement
  • Financial and transaction records: Retained for up to 7 years to comply with applicable tax and accounting laws
  • Legal hold data: Retained as required by law, court order, or regulatory obligation
11

Children's Privacy

TechIT Network is not directed at individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If we become aware that a user is under 18 or that a minor has submitted personal data, we will promptly delete it and close the account. If you believe a minor has registered, please contact us at anthony@techitnetwork.com.

12

Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as our platform evolves or as laws change. When we make significant changes:

  • We will update the effective date at the top of this page
  • Registered users will be notified via email at least 14 days before changes take effect
  • For material changes requiring fresh consent, we will present an explicit consent prompt on login

Continued use of TechIT Network after the effective date of any update constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
